Calibration result

Household types

Income median: gross household income. Wealth median: non-pension net wealth among households that have any. Spreads are lognormal σ. Pension wealth (BVG, 3a) is added on top, computed from age and income.

How it works

  • Revenue target: consolidated tax-financed spending of Confederation, cantons and communes, net of non-tax revenue, plus the VAT share currently paid into AHV. Social insurances stay on the payroll.
  • Population: 28 household types (age × single/couple × children). Each type gets a synthetic sample with correlated income and wealth (lognormal body, shared Pareto tail). Weights match household counts.
  • Model A: marginal rate t(x) = tmax·(1 − (x₀/x)k) on equivalised income (OECD-modified scale 1 / 0.5 / 0.3), taxable income = gross − employee social contributions. The solver finds tmax.
  • Model B: rate falls from rhigh at Wlow to rlow at Whigh, on net wealth including BVG and 3a capital (present value of the BVG annuity after 65). Tax below Tmin is dropped; tax is capped at Tmax and kept non-decreasing in wealth. The solver scales both rates by one factor.
  • Static: nobody changes behaviour. Treat results as orders of magnitude.
